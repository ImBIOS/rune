- **Playground: folding groups.** A click on a group's header folds or unfolds it, and so do ←, → and ↵ while the header is the list's selection. ↑↓ walk headers and the stories that show; J K skip the headers. Groups stay folded until the playground closes. ← leaves the side column, as Tab and Esc do. `Playground::scenes(|name| ..)` picks which of `AppSpec::scenes` the list shows. Choosing a scene, or a story whose state is new, runs its `'r'` trigger as R does, so its motion plays at once, headless `--playground NAME` included. `Playground::fresh(|real| ..)` starts scenes from the real state, and `Story::from_real(name, |real| .., view)` derives a story's state from it, each time it's made.
